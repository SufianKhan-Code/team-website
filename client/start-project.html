After You Book

What happens to your request?

A project booking is the starting point for scope review — not an instant contract or automatic payment.

01

Brief review

We review your business goal, website type, pages, functionality and technical setup.

02

Clarification

If anything important is missing, we contact you before finalizing scope.

03

Scope & quote

The final deliverables, price, milestones and estimated timeline are defined.

04

Confirmation

Development starts after both sides agree on the project scope and working terms.
