How We Work

A workflow built to reduce confusion.

Instead of jumping straight into code, we define the problem, scope the work and create the right structure first. That keeps design, development and feedback moving in the same direction.

Clear scopeVisible milestonesRegular feedbackQuality checks
01
Discover

Understand the business, users and goal.

We start by understanding what you want to build, who will use it and what success should look like.

We discussBusiness goals, audience, required features, references, deadline and budget range.
We clarifyWhat is essential for launch and what can be planned for a later phase.
You provideExisting brand assets, content, examples, access details and any current system information.
OUTPUTProject briefGoals · users · priorities
02
Scope & Plan

Turn the idea into a buildable plan.

Once the goal is clear, we break the project into pages, modules, workflows and delivery milestones.

StructurePages, user roles, features, dashboard areas and major user flows are listed.
EstimatePricing and timeline are confirmed after the final scope is understood.
MilestonesThe work is organized into practical checkpoints so progress stays easy to review.
OUTPUTScope & roadmapFeatures · milestones · quote
03
UI/UX Design

Shape the experience before development.

For projects that need design work, we organize content, define hierarchy and build interfaces around the user journey.

WireframeKey sections and screen structure are planned before visual polish.
Visual directionTypography, spacing, components, cards, forms and responsive behavior are defined.
ReviewYou review the direction before development moves into the main implementation phase.
OUTPUTApproved UI directionLayouts · flows · components
04
Development

Build the product in working stages.

We turn the approved plan into a responsive, functional product and connect the required frontend, backend and data workflows.

FrontendResponsive interfaces, interactions, forms, dashboards and reusable components.
BackendAPIs, authentication, permissions, business logic and integrations when required.
DatabaseData structures and application flows are connected according to project requirements.
OUTPUTWorking buildFrontend · backend · data
05
Test & Refine

Check the details before launch.

Before handoff, we review the product across the key flows and devices and fix issues that affect usability or delivery quality.

Functional testingForms, links, user flows, authentication and important actions are checked.
Responsive testingDesktop, tablet and mobile layouts are reviewed for spacing, overflow and interaction issues.
Final polishVisual inconsistencies, content issues and approved final refinements are handled.
OUTPUTLaunch-ready productTested · refined · approved
06
Launch & Support

Deploy, hand over and keep it healthy.

We take the approved version live, confirm the production setup and support the next steps after launch.

DeploymentThe product is deployed to the agreed hosting environment and production settings are checked.
HandoverRelevant access, source files and project information are organized for the client.
Ongoing supportBug fixes, improvements, new features and maintenance can continue as a separate support service.
OUTPUTLive productDeploy · handoff · support
Communication

You should always know what is happening.

Good delivery is not only code. We keep decisions, feedback and next steps visible so the project does not become a black box.

01

One clear point of direction

Requirements and feedback are kept focused so implementation does not split into conflicting directions.

02

Milestone reviews

Important stages are reviewed before the next major phase, reducing expensive late changes.

03

Feedback with context

We prefer specific feedback tied to the business goal, page, feature or user flow being reviewed.

04

New requests that expand scope are separated from the original agreed work before implementation.

Before We Start

Common process questions.

Every project is different, but these are the rules we use to keep delivery practical and predictable.

Do all projects go through every phase?+

No. A small bug fix or deployment task may not need discovery and design at the same depth as a custom business application.

When is the final price confirmed?+

After the requirements and scope are clear. More users, modules, reports, integrations, automation or revisions can increase the project scope and price.

Can the scope change during development?+

Yes, but new requests are reviewed separately so their impact on price and timeline is clear before they are added.

Do you support the project after launch?+

Yes. Ongoing bug fixes, feature work, deployment help and maintenance can continue under the support and maintenance service.
