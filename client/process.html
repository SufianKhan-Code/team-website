Before We Start

Common questions.

Every project is different, but these rules keep delivery predictable.

Do all projects use all six stages?+

No. Small fixes or deployment tasks may use a shorter version of the process.

When is the final price confirmed?+

After the requirements and scope are clear. More modules, integrations, users or revisions can increase the quote.

Can scope change during development?+

Yes. New requests are reviewed separately so the impact on price and timeline is clear first.

Do you support the project after launch?+

Yes. Maintenance, fixes and future features can continue as a separate support service.
