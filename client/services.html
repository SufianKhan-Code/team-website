Pricing rules

What determines the final quote?

These rules are taken directly from the pricing guide and keep project quotes consistent.

01

Lead with outcomes

Sell the business result and features first rather than leading with programming languages, frameworks or database names.

02

Collect requirements

Complete business requirements should be collected before a final quote is given.

03

Scope changes price

More users, branches, modules, reports, integrations and automation increase the project price.

04

Quote the complete solution

Complex business software should account for UI/UX, frontend, backend, database, testing and deployment.
